## Reusable selection correction (P13A)

P12J's previous `current`, `progressionCurrent`, and `priceCurrent` flags described freshness relative to an archived capture, not the live game. They are now named `captureCurrent`, `progressionMatchesCapture`, and `priceMatchesCapture`; none claims that a live root, progression, or price still matches. The reusable evaluator no longer excludes an owner solely because it is responding. P12G's same-boundary plan remains eligible for comparison, while the absent incremental constraint benefit and incomplete external commitments prevent an admitted request. The selector reports executable possibilities in input order, with no selected ID or strategic tie until comparable utility evidence exists. It records structural validity, supported effect, usefulness, preference, and execution authorization separately. P12J remains test-only and its historical intervention conclusion is unchanged.

## Controlled result reused from P12G

P12G ran two identical native-baseline A sessions and one isolated alternative B from the same export, seed, epoch, settings, and pinned game. Only B used the Steel-oriented `productionSmelting` policy; the normal Smelter owner and other owners remained enabled. Its native `iSmelter` controls performed the assignment change. The exact observed start was 15 Smelters, 15 Coal fuelers, 15 Iron, 0 Steel; the B allocation changed to 12 Iron and 3 Steel initially and ended at 3 Iron / 12 Steel, while the baseline ended at 5 Iron / 10 Steel.

The action was 9,000 Knowledge and 250 Steel. Both arms first met the Steel threshold at period 40 and invoked Research at period 72. P12H’s freshness check distinguishes cached affordability from a current draw: at period 70 a protected fresh draw preserved root, progression, price and saved economic state, and reported current affordability. That evidence prevents treating the older cached false verdict as a current threshold. The intervention did not change this fresh threshold or the next native action time. No terminal MAD-time effect is inferred.

`compareGoalStrategies()` received the paired finite-window marginal and known 1,000-Steel Construction commitment. It returned no winner because complete external commitments and stable production forecasts were unavailable. The measured additional Steel therefore establishes a resource increase, not an overall goal improvement.
