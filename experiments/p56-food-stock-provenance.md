# P56 — Native Food-stock discontinuity provenance

**Decision: A — Source identified and reproduced.** The transitions to exactly 50 are produced by the existing automation `Gather` path invoking the pinned game's ordinary Food harvest action 50 times when its Food stock is empty. Each call adds one Food for this Standard Human replay. The +50 is a batch of 50 native `modRes("Food", 1, true)` mutations, not a native fixed-50 grant, starvation reward, market transfer, or sampling artifact.

No production allocation, Jobs policy, Construction authority, resource behavior, or native-game checkout was changed. P56 only adds an experiment harness, a temporary instrumented copy of the pinned native snapshot, regressions, and evidence. The independent renderer CPU gate remains unresolved.

## Food modeling and remaining boundary

P56 does not add a +50 residual or update P45's pure stock model. A valid projection still needs separate, current inputs for:

1. Native production and consumption, including the current fast-loop multiplier and pool mode.
2. Current stored Food and its capacity.
3. External Food grants and drains, including the active Gather policy/click limit, player/native actions, events, market trades, and any pool transfers.
4. Weather and other current production modifiers.
5. The actual starvation random outcome and resulting population change.
6. Worker commitments, native job bounds, and assignment authority.

The P45 replay establishes the provenance of this discontinuity, not the safety of a three-Farm/three-Farmer/one-Quarry allocation. In p5632, Food is 50 while the retained net rate is still −2.25; the recovery batch can follow a starvation event and says nothing about later commitment, event, weather, capacity, or assignment changes. Do not declare an autonomous Jobs allocation Food-safe from this result.
