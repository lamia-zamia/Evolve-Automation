# P44 — Food infrastructure dependency and military-demand feasibility

## Decision

**C — Insufficient economic capacity under the tested demand and modifier range.** Three Farms / three Farmers / one Quarry Worker is a valid _point-in-time_ projection at the selected high-demand p4000 state: 8.388 Food against demand 8, a +0.388 margin, with Stone headroom and a native-eligible Quarry Worker. It is not robust across the retained P43 B timeline. At p5768, retained Weather is −30%; the same plan projects a −2.044 Food margin. Even three Farms / four Farmers / zero Quarry Workers is −1.393 there. The bounded Farm/Jobs alternatives therefore do not demonstrate sustained positive Food and productive Stone across the observed conditions.

Construction and worker authority are independently blocked as well. The second Farm has no Lumber available in the retained state, Construction later saves for University, and the current worker-commitment source is unavailable. These constraints make the favorable p4000 projection non-actionable. No Farm was purchased, no Jobs allocation or military behavior changed, and no resources or Gather authority were granted.

At four soldiers, hunting returns about 1.333 Food while the soldiers add four Food of consumption, so the army contributes a net 2.667 Food burden in these samples. Hunting is already included in every P44 projection; it does not cover the demand increase.

Garrison growth is native automatic training against available Garrison capacity. The tick advances training progress while workers are below capacity and adds a worker on completion ([pinned training loop](../test-artifacts/game/evolve-deadspace.js:155233)). A Garrison building adds capacity for two soldiers below Military V; the observed two buildings support four workers ([pinned Garrison capacity](../test-artifacts/game/evolve-deadspace.js:112972)). P43 records one Garrison building by p1264 and two by p2624 in A, then four Garrison workers by p3024. B reaches one building by p3064, two by p3480, and four soldiers by p3880.

This is not an economic planner assigning Garrison workers from the civilian pool, and no P42/P43 policy suppresses training. Construction acquired the capacity through its existing native path; P43 retained building counts and saving targets, not a per-purchase Garrison trace. Thus the training is automatic once capacity exists, while the precise Garrison building candidate decisions are not fully attributable from the retained trace. No separate player training toggle or authorized military-demand suppression path was established.

Food reaches zero at p1589 in A and p5626 in B. Native starvation first increments at p1589 and p5630, respectively; the first Jobs observations showing those increments are p1592 and p5632. Neither arm records deaths. Agriculture II is present at p728 and Agriculture III by p1584; Military I is present by p1584. Weather changes independently of the military demand, including −30% in the later B timeline. These output modifiers affect supply, while Garrison growth explains the increase in consumption. See the P43 [transition table and attribution](p43-food-safe-jobs-validation.md#stage-1--food-collapse-attribution) and [retained observations](p43-food-safe-jobs-timeline.json).

## Stage 2 — Farm and Jobs projections

The projection independently reapplies the pinned Farmer slot formula to each retained P43 B state:

```text
farmerBase = min(Farms, Farmers) × (0.82 + fullFarmBonus)
           + max(0, Farmers − Farms) × 0.82
foodOutput = effectiveOtherFoodProduction
           + farmerBase × observedFarmerFormulaCoefficient
foodMargin = foodOutput − currentFoodConsumption
positive   = foodMargin > 0.01
```

The native Farmer impact is 0.82. A full Farm slot adds 1.15 from Agriculture II onward; Farmers above slot count are farmhands and receive only 0.82 ([Farmer impact and bonus](../test-artifacts/game/evolve-deadspace.js:104296), [Agriculture modifier](../test-artifacts/game/evolve-deadspace.js:105580), [Farm slots / farmhands](../test-artifacts/game/evolve-deadspace.js:150653)). For each observation, the P43 source reconstruction supplies the current effective Farmer coefficient, including Mill, Virgo, Weather, Quarantine and global production modifiers, plus the effective separately modeled Soldier output. P43’s observed output is reconciled to the native Food net and all consumer rows before this projection runs. No active non-Farmer / non-Soldier Food producer is present in the retained sample.

Only the Farm slot count and Farmer allocation change hypothetically. The current interval’s source-backed modifiers and demand are held fixed. This is conditional on construction not changing those same-period modifiers; no hypothetical Farm was built or replayed. The captured source path makes Farm count affect the Farmer slot split, while the production factors are separately retained per state. Weather is not frozen to one constant: each row uses the observed period’s Weather and modifier reconstruction.

All table values use the current demand at that exact P43 B observation and the source-reconstructed modifiers from that same observation. The 3F / 3 Farm calculation at p4000 is `5.91 × 1.194706236 + 1.327451374 = 8.388165`, not an extrapolation from the four-Farmer result. The 3-Farmer base is `3 × (0.82 + 1.15) = 5.91`; all three Farmers use full Farm slots. For comparison, four Farmers across three Farms use three full slots plus one farmhand: base `3 × 1.97 + 0.82 = 6.73`, giving 9.368 Food. With two Farms and four Farmers, the base is 5.58 and p4000 output is only 7.994, a −0.006 margin that fails the strict +0.01 threshold.

At the selected periods, 3 Farms / 3 Farmers / 1 Quarry Worker conserves all four civilians and gives positive Food in all five snapshots. At p4000, Quarry ownership is present, Stone is 59.164 / 600, and the captured Quarry Worker smart maximum is unbounded; a one-worker Quarry assignment therefore has native output eligibility and storage headroom. The 3-Farmer plan exceeds the captured Farmer smart maximum of two, although the physical job maximum is unlimited and the plan fits the four residents. The unchanged ordinary planner does not make that assignment. P43 labels worker commitments unavailable and its diagnostic branch explicitly assumes an empty set; this makes the projection non-authoritative.

The full B timeline is less favorable than the selected checkpoints. Of 751 available observations with Food demand 8, the 3 Farms / 3 Farmers plan has a strict positive margin in 455. At p5768, Agriculture III remains present, Weather is −30%, Food demand is 8, Stone is 63.749 / 600, and the projection is only 5.956 Food: **−2.044 margin**, even though one Quarry Worker could produce into the available Stone headroom. The maximum-Food 3 Farms / 4 Farmers plan is also negative there (−1.393) and leaves no Quarry Worker. More than three Farms cannot improve a three-Farmer plan because all three full Farm slots are already used. Therefore the p4000 positive margin is a point result; it is not robust over the retained demand and Weather conditions.

## Stage 4 — Worker-commitment ownership

The production architecture has no separate authoritative, complete worker-commitment registry. The captured ordinary Jobs cycle observes its current managed-job catalog and assignments; `hasCompleteJobCatalog` checks for missing managed controls. The full-Jobs preparation also samples Foundry workers and crafting targets, then joins them to the ordinary input using the same catalog closure rather than drawing a second catalog ([full Jobs preparation](../src/adapters/evolve/civic/captured-ordinary-jobs.ts:681), [cycle preparation and freshness](../src/adapters/evolve/civic/captured-ordinary-jobs.ts:1744)). Native `reserveMiner` is a specific race- and condition-driven reservation, not a general registry ([capture](../src/adapters/evolve/civic/captured-job-catalog.ts:1008), [Jobs planner](../src/domain/civic/jobs.ts:491)). The captured worker-progress source records completed work, not future worker allocations.

Thus the prepared full Jobs cycle can represent the current ordinary assignments, Foundry/crafting work, and specific miner reservation. It does not establish a complete independent set of all possible owner-held worker claims. A 3F / 1Q transfer could be invalidated by an allocation not represented by that cycle. P43’s p720 evidence has `autoCraftsmen: false`, no crafting target, zero Foundry maximum and `reserveMiner: false`, so no competing allocation was observed in that particular sample. The P43 policy nevertheless correctly leaves commitments unavailable and marks its empty set as test-only.

Any future read-only authority check must reuse the prepared full Jobs sample with its exact work-cycle period/sequence, root and progression generations, and Farmer / Quarry control generations. The Foundry sample is already joined to that prepared cycle; no additional native catalog draw is required. The missing contract is a complete, freshness-bound commitment view with an explicit `known`/`unavailable` state. Until that contract exists, absence must remain unavailable rather than becoming an authoritative empty set.

## Stage 5 — Capacity-aware Stone objective

P37’s current diagnostic goal requires positive Stone production as a terminal condition ([P37 objective](p37-economic-goal-dependencies.md#dependency-model)). That is too strict when the Stone store is full: the native Quarry Worker smart maximum falls to zero, and zero production with no Quarry Worker is correct. P42/P43 show the Stone store at its 600 cap and ordinary Jobs releasing Quarry Workers.

P44 defines a separate read-only predicate in `evaluateP44CapacityAwareStoneObjective()` in the P44 experiment code. It is not wired into runtime or substituted for P37:

```text
require fresh, same-sample Food, population, Stone, and Jobs evidence
foodSafe = complete Food sources and Food production − consumption > 0.01
populationSafe = housed population and no starvation/death increment
stoneUseful = storage headroom > 0 OR fresh uncovered Stone demand > 0

pass when foodSafe AND populationSafe AND
  (NOT stoneUseful OR
   current authorized Jobs evidence has a Quarry Worker,
   native Quarry output per worker is positive, and Quarry is owned)
```

Evidence must share the same period, Jobs work-cycle sequence, root generation and progression generation. When replenishment is needed, Jobs owner generation and both job-control generations must also be current; Stone demand and any price / reservation evidence must be current. If Stone is full, the demand source must be known and complete before treating demand as zero. Missing or mismatched provenance, unknown demand at full storage, unavailable commitments, or unsupported Quarry output returns `unavailable`; it never grants spending or assignment authority. Focused tests cover full storage with no demand, headroom, positive authorized Quarry output, stale provenance and unavailable worker authority. P37 itself is unchanged.

## Stage 7 — Performance and verification

The analyzer reads retained JSON only. It makes no browser or game call, introduces no recurring planner work, adds no native catalog draw, creates no Worker, and does not change production behavior. Its pure projection and aggregation loops took **6.857 ms** for **10,494 scenario evaluations**. The normalized input was **1,528,211 bytes** across **1,161** observations. Timing uses Node’s native monotonic `process.hrtime.bigint()` and excludes file I/O, JSON parsing and normalization. These figures are not a renderer CPU percentage; the independent 2% CPU requirement remains unresolved.

An eventual integration should reuse the prepared same-cycle full Jobs sample and its Foundry / control generations. It needs one same-root Food breakdown with complete producer, consumer and modifier rows (P43 measured one additional Food breakdown read per Jobs cycle), plus same-cycle Stone amount/capacity and a complete useful-demand / reservation observation when Stone is full or a purchase is being considered. It must not draw the Jobs catalog a second time. The external worker-commitment contract remains missing.

Focused P44 regressions cover Farm slots and farmhands, changing Weather / modifier coefficients, military Food demand, Stone capacity and Quarry eligibility, strict margins, population conservation, source identity, unavailable commitments and the capacity-aware objective. P44 does not change the pinned native source, P37 diagnostic goal, P41 reservation gate, Construction priorities, Jobs executor, military behavior or persisted settings.
