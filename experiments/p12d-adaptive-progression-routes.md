## Graph limitation and separate meanings

`planNativeResearchPrerequisites()` retains the effective declared action and requirement edges. When it expands an unoffered action, it also adds the unevaluated native queue-precognition and game-specific exception as a reason. That makes the action and dependent requirement gates `conditional`, even where the declared ancestry is known. `classifyMandatoryNativeResearchObligations()` then correctly declines to prove a mandatory frontier across those conditional alternatives. The strict classifier and its authority have not been weakened.

P12D traverses the effective declarations separately. It treats current offer membership as current actionability, while future offer membership, native conditions, route restrictions, partial dynamic requirements, missing grants, and excluded routes remain separate evidence. It never converts an unknown to true or false. The advisory output explicitly reports `mandatoryObligation: not-certified` and `authorizationToSpend: false` for every candidate.

The generic linear and cross-technology tests demonstrate the graph distinction: the strict graph gates remain conditional through unevaluated native exceptions, while the advisory model can still expose the current offered frontier and its declared chain. Tests also cover AND branches, OR alternatives, shared nodes, exclusions, missing conditions, cycles, invalidation and changing price, production, capacity, and grant levels.
